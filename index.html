How It Began

Our Story

21Shows since Dec 2024
8Venues
2Cities

Freshly minted in Mumbai.
We went live in December 2024, at Cobbler & Crew, Pune.
We haven't stopped since. 21 shows. 8 venues. 2 cities.
The Congregation grows every night, one juke joint at a time.

Our songbook comprises a mix of our original progressive blues and soul songs about anxiety, love, loss, compassion, unity and the beauty of life itself; and covers of iconic numbers whose songwriting and rhythms have inspired us. Our debut album is on the way!

It's Church.It's Therapy.It's The Blues.
